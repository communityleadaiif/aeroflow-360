# ✈️ AeroFlow 360 — Autonomous Aircraft Turnaround & Smart Ramp Orchestration Matrix
### *TechRaga '26 Hackathon | Theme 5: Smart Business Management / Institution Automation*
**Presented by**: BBA Aviation Management Students

---

## 🏆 Project Overview

**AeroFlow 360** is an enterprise-grade, real-time Airport Operations Control Center (AOCC) and autonomous ramp workflow dispatch platform. It is engineered to solve aviation's most expensive operational crisis: **Turnaround Time (TAT) delays**.

By replacing outdated walkie-talkie logs, handwritten ramp sheets, and verbal phone checks with a **synchronized 8-milestone digital critical path**, AeroFlow 360 delivers zero-delay ground turnarounds, automated SLA breach escalation, and complete digital auditability compliant with **IATA Airport Handling Manual (AHM 730/780)**.

## 🎛️ Key Technical & Operational Features

1. **Airport Operations Control Center (AOCC)**: Live gate matrix (G1–G8), active flight status queue, and telemetry clock.
2. **Synchronized 8-Milestone Critical Path Engine**:
   - Chocks On & Deboarding (0–5 min)
   - Baggage Offloading (3–12 min)
   - Cabin Grooming & Security (8–20 min)
   - ATF Refueling & Digital Fuel Slip (10–24 min)
   - In-flight Catering Replenishment (12–25 min)
   - Potable Water & Lavatory (15–26 min)
   - Baggage Loading & Trim Sheet (20–30 min)
   - Passenger Boarding & Pushback Tug (25–35 min)
3. **Interactive Hackathon Simulator**: One-click simulation of optimal turns, catering delays, and digital fuel chit handshakes.
4. **Ground Support Equipment (GSE) Fleet Allocator**: Battery/fuel tracking for Pushback Tugs, Belt Loaders, and Bowsers.
5. **Interactive Aviation ROI Calculator**: Dynamic sliders computing ₹ Crores saved, fuel reduction, and carbon offset.
6. **IATA Clearance Certificate Generator**: Print-ready turnaround summary compliant with IATA AHM 730/780.
7. **Built-in Jury Pitch Deck**: On-screen slide presentation mode accessible with a single click.
